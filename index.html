Tiny Desk AI Video: notes on a fixed-template band video generator

Field notes from testing Tiny Desk AI Video, an AI band video generator. Written for people building something similar, not as marketing.

The problem is the cast, not the model

A "band video" template is five independent portrait slots composited into one staged performance: back left, back right, keys, lead and sax. Two of those slots sit behind the others, so occlusion order decides whether a generated portrait reads as a band member or as a sticker pasted on top.

The design decision that matters most is making every slot optional. An empty slot falls back to a fixed dog character, which means the tool works with zero uploads. That sounds like a small convenience. It is actually what makes the first run fast enough to keep anyone's attention.

Limits worth stating before someone hits them

  • Five photos, JPG, PNG or WebP, 8 MB each.
  • Formats: 9:16 vertical, 1:1 square, 16:9 landscape. 720p or 1080p.
  • No 4:5. Saying so is cheaper than letting people discover it in an error state.
  • The prompt and the music are fixed by the template. Pretending otherwise creates support load for a feature that does not exist.
  • Celebrity and public-figure photos are out, and no permission checkbox overrides that.

Show the credit cost before the click

Show the exact credit cost for the current format and quality selection, not a range. A range reads as a lie the first time the number comes in higher.

Generation is slow, so hand back a reference

A band video takes minutes, not seconds. Returning a request reference lets the user close the tab, come back later and check status instead of watching a progress bar. Videos are kept for 30 days, and the guidance says to download promptly — both should be visible before the first run, not after.

What this is not

It is a performance generator, not a video editor. You choose optional cast photos, format and quality; everything else is set. That constraint is the product. Trying to open up the prompt would turn a two-minute task into a blank timeline.
